An Investigation of Language Models Using Prompts

Abstract
Large language models are powerful. We investigate them using prompts. We tried
several prompts and report what happened. Our findings show that prompts matter.

1. Introduction
Language models such as GPT are widely used. Prompting is a technique where you
give the model text. In this work we investigate prompting. This is an important
topic because many people use prompts.

2. Related Work
Other people have studied prompting. We are aware of this literature.

3. Approach
We wrote ten prompts by hand. We gave the prompts to a language model. We looked
at the outputs and judged whether they were good. One author did the judging.
We did not define criteria in advance.

4. Findings
Some prompts produced better outputs than other prompts. Longer prompts
sometimes helped. Shorter prompts sometimes helped too. It depends. We did not
measure statistical significance because the sample was small.

5. Discussion
Our findings suggest prompts are important. This agrees with intuition. There
are limitations: we used one model, ten prompts, and one annotator. We did not
release our prompts.

6. Conclusion
Prompting matters. Future work should investigate further.
